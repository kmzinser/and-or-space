about the gallery space

( and / or / space ) is housed in a 1950 building that was the home of a printing company for 60+ years. It was redesigned and renovated by curb, a Knoxville-based architectural firm that has earned numerous local, regional, and national design awards. In 2023, the front 800 square feet of the space was partitioned off and redesigned to be a short-term rental property with artwork installed throughout the space, particularly in the front room.

Based on feedback from guests, the idea for the gallery was born. The space opens to the public for events, and during the more than 60% of the time it isn't booked, exhibitions are on view by appointment. Our exhibitions are up for several months rather than several weeks. We take no commission on sales—it all goes directly to the artist. We consciously schedule our opening receptions on the LAST FRIDAY of the month so that more folks can join our gatherings.
